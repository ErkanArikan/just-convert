# LibreOffice notice

Just Convert embeds LibreOffice 26.8.1, an official build published by The Document Foundation, to convert Office documents to PDF. LibreOffice is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0, with portions under other open-source licenses.

The embedded copy is otherwise unmodified. On Linux, optional Qt, KDE, GTK 4, and Java desktop-integration modules are removed. Its complete license texts and third-party notices ship inside it:

- macOS: `vendor/libreoffice/LibreOffice.app/Contents/Resources/LICENSE.html` and `NOTICE`
- Linux: `vendor/libreoffice/LICENSE.html` and `NOTICE`
- Windows: `vendor/libreoffice/LICENSE.html` and `NOTICE`

Source code is available from The Document Foundation at https://www.libreoffice.org/about-us/source-code/. License overview: https://www.libreoffice.org/licenses/.
